Portfolio Ranking Method

A growth portfolio built from Y Combinator startups, with a reason for every dollar.

A rule-based model scores each startup and splits the capital across the top ten, keeping a cash reserve. Each pick is matched to a listed company with real financials and checked against news and community sentiment.

Ranking

    Method

    Score

    The dataset has no revenue or valuation figures, so each factor uses the closest observable proxy. Each factor is scaled to 0–1.

      Allocation

      • Only operating companies are eligible; the ten highest scores are picked.
      • 10% of capital is held in cash.
      • Weights tilt toward better-funded, more widely backed companies (log-scaled), then are capped between 4% and 25%.
      • Code does the maths; the LLM only explains and matches comparables.

      Pipeline

      1. score_startups.py scores and allocates
      2. match_comparables.py picks 3 listed peers (LLM)
      3. fetch_finnhub.py pulls their financials
      4. analyze_sentiment.py reads news and Hacker News
      5. web/build_data.py builds this page's data

      Sources

      • Startups and founders: Y Combinator dataset, 2005–2014 cohorts
      • Public comparables: Finnhub and SEC filings
      • Sentiment: Google News, Hacker News, LLM summary

      Educational screening tool, not financial advice. Startups can't be bought directly; listed comparables are context, not substitutes.
